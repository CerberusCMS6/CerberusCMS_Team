What is a Native American ?
Native Americans are any person who biologically descends from people who lived within the continents of what is now widely known as America before the arrival of any other peoples such as the Europeans... foster-ship of a non-Native American into a Native American Tribe does not make that person a Native American unless they marry in and have offspring... Only biologically descended people from biological Native Americans are actual Native Americans.

Who can prove that they are a Native American ?
Documentation cannot prove that a Native American is a Native American.. only the natural adult teeth can prove that a Native American is a Native American or that a person is part Native American.
